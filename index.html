I / ABOUT THE SERVER

MORE THAN A
KINGDOM.

Crownfall Kingdoms is a Minecraft Java and Bedrock server where every decision can change the balance of power.

Create or join a kingdom, gather resources, build your capital, trade with other players, form alliances, and take part in official wars and events. Coins are used for trading, Kingdom Power decides the season champion, and reputation reflects how reliably a kingdom keeps its agreements.

IV / CROWNFALL BOT

YOUR KINGDOM
TOOLS.

Useful commands help keep kingdom life organised.

♛
01 / CREATE

/create_team

Start an application for your own kingdom. Follow the prompts and provide the requested kingdom information for staff review.

♟
02 / MEMBERS

Manage your roster

Kingdom leaders can manage members and leadership roles. Use the available member commands to keep your roster up to date.

⚖
03 / ECONOMY

Trade and diplomacy

The bot also supports kingdom information, economy statistics, trade agreements, reports, and official diplomacy workflows.

Available commands may depend on your Discord permissions. Check the bot command list in Discord for the current options.
